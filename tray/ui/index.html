Where do I get a client ID?
  1. Open the Spotify dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app, then open its Settings.
  3. Under Redirect URIs paste http://127.0.0.1:8899/callback, click Add, then Save.
  4. Copy the Client ID, paste it above, hit Save. Your browser opens, you click Agree, done.

Azeradio

A soundtrack for Azeroth that follows you.

Every zone below has its own playlist, built track by track for that stretch of the world: ten songs in the order you would walk them, no track used twice. Press play and hear a zone before you ever set foot in it.

In game, Azeradio plays them for you. A small addon notes when you cross a zone line, a tray app follows along, and Spotify changes with the landscape. Combat gets its own music. So does coming home.

More zones are added one at a time, as they are traveled. The library is the content; Azeradio is the delivery.

Playlists are free to hear. The tray app needs Spotify Premium. Azeradio is ToS-safe: the game writes its own chat log, and the app only talks to Spotify's official API.
